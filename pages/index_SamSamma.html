NOW HAPPENING · 園區日誌Field Journal

五月，攜手籌備前往日本的竹工藝展May: preparing together for a bamboo-craft exhibition in Japan

2026 年 5 月｜大隘部落紀錄May 2026 | Daai Tribe journal

最近，園區正為了一項即將遠行的計畫而充滿期待。透過明新科大的計畫推動，我們即將把臺灣優美的竹藝品帶往日本交流。 Lately the site has been full of anticipation for a journey ahead. Through a program led by Minghsin University of Science and Technology (MUST), we are about to bring Taiwan's beautiful bamboo crafts to Japan for exchange.

這次展演匯聚了跨界的力量，由技藝精湛的專業工藝師傾注心力操刀。看著他們熟練地將竹篾交織， 展現深厚的工藝底蘊，園區的夥伴們則化身幕後推手，從旁協助籌劃與連結資源。這是一場串聯技藝與心意的合作 —— 我們將這份職人精神帶上飛機，帶去京都，帶給願意看見的世界。 This showcase brings together cross-disciplinary forces, crafted with devotion by master artisans. Watching them skillfully weave bamboo strips, revealing deep craftsmanship, the site's members became the behind-the-scenes hands — helping plan and connect resources. It is a collaboration of skill and heart —— we carry this artisan spirit onto the plane, to Kyoto, to a world willing to see.

五月底，這四件作品將在京都府龜岡市的工藝展，被遠方的人輕輕拿起、慢慢端詳。 In late May, these four works will be gently picked up and admired by people far away, at a craft exhibition in Kameoka, Kyoto.

Our Story · 我們的足跡Our Path

從族人發起，到走向世界的這條路From the people, to the world — the path we walked

這不是一份「我們的優勢」清單。 是三段被時間留下的真實腳印， 從部落、走進大學、走到京都。 This isn't a list of "our strengths." It's three real footprints left by time — from the tribe, into the university, all the way to Kyoto.

FOUNDER · 部落自主經營Tribe-run
第一段 · 族人Part One · The People
By the People, of the Land

族人，是這個地方真正的主人Our people are the true owners of this place

山莎蔓岸是部落自己成立的合作社， 發起人是世代居住於大隘部落的賽夏族人。 所有獲利留在部落、所有故事都是親身經歷 — 我們不會聘演員、也沒有表演動作， 你看到的是這片土地上真正在生活的人。 SamSamma is a co-op founded by the tribe itself, started by Saisiyat people who have lived in the Daai tribe for generations. All profit stays in the tribe, and every story is lived firsthand — we hire no actors and stage no performances; what you see are real people who actually live on this land.

四位族人取得專業證照， 文化傳承不是給遊客看的劇場，是日常的師徒制傳承。 Four community members hold professional licenses; cultural heritage here isn't theater for tourists, but everyday master-to-apprentice transmission.

2024 年依《原住民族基本法》正式立案Officially registered in 2024 under the Indigenous Peoples Basic Act
USR · 大學走進部落University meets tribe
第二段 · 學生Part Two · The Students
Students In, Co-op Out

學生走進部落，部落走進大學Students step into the tribe; the tribe steps into the university

從 2024 年開始，明新科大的學生陸續來到這裡 — 他們蹲在地上削竹篾、爬上樹屋鋪屋頂、 在會議室裡跟族人一起報告心得。 Since 2024, students from MUST have come here one after another — crouching on the ground to shave bamboo strips, climbing tree houses to lay roofs, and presenting reflections alongside the tribe in the meeting room.

我們和明新科大整合了 永續設計、文創、商管、原民研究四個系所的老師， 把竹林變成大學社會責任實踐基地 (USR)。 這不是來打卡的場域，是被列入課程的真實學習現場。 Together with MUST we brought in faculty from four departments — sustainable design, cultural-creative industries, business management, and indigenous studies — turning the bamboo forest into a University Social Responsibility (USR) base. It's not a check-in spot, but a real learning site written into the curriculum.

JAPAN · 走向世界Going global
第三段 · 走向世界Part Three · Going Global
From Daai Village to Kyoto

我們的竹編，正在出國Our bamboo weaving is going abroad

2026 年 5 月，族人親自帶著作品飛到日本京都， 在當地工藝展現場用賽夏族傳統技法展示給日本朋友看。 這些不是「複製品」、不是「禮物」， 是部落師傅在大隘的竹林裡用整個四月編出來的作品。 In May 2026, our people personally flew their works to Kyoto, Japan, demonstrating Saisiyat traditional techniques to Japanese friends at a local craft exhibition. These are not "replicas" or "gifts" — they are works the tribe's masters wove over the whole of April, in the bamboo forests of Daai.

同年，京都府龜岡市簽署了合作意向書 — 我們和京都的竹工藝師傅， 開始討論一場可能持續好多年的交流。 That same year, Kameoka, Kyoto signed a letter of intent — and we and Kyoto's bamboo-craft masters began discussing an exchange that may last for many years.

預約前最常被問到的 6 個問題The 6 questions we hear most before a visit

現在可以購買竹炭產品嗎？Can I buy bamboo-charcoal products now?
目前竹炭產線仍在升級建置中（115-116 年度高溫窯體升級至 900-1000°C）， 預計 116 年度（2027 年）正式量產並開放預購。 建設期間可預訂「企業 ESG 採購提案」，由合作社報價並安排首批產品。 The charcoal line is still being upgraded (high-temperature kiln raised to 900–1000°C in 2026–2027), with full production and pre-orders expected to open in 2027. During construction you can reserve a "corporate ESG procurement proposal," and the co-op will quote and arrange the first batch.
可以隨時參觀基地嗎？Can I visit the site anytime?
目前基地建設仍在進行，為了你的安全與導覽品質，所有參訪都需事先預約。 這也讓我們能依你的需求安排專業族人導覽，而非自助瀏覽。 The site is still under construction, so for your safety and tour quality, all visits require advance booking. This also lets us arrange a professional tribal guide tailored to your needs, rather than self-guided browsing.
體驗費用如何計算？How are fees calculated?
費用依據方案、人數、時長與附加需求（餐食 / 住宿 / 接駁 / 保險）綜合報價。 預算範圍可於表單中標示，合作社將提供透明報價單； 學校教育價、企業價、客製價分流計算。 Fees are quoted based on the plan, group size, duration, and add-ons (meals / lodging / shuttle / insurance). You can note your budget range in the form, and the co-op provides a transparent quote; school, corporate, and custom rates are calculated separately.
如何取消或變更預約？How do I cancel or change a booking?
活動 7 天前可免費變更或取消； 3-7 天內取消酌收 30% 行政費； 72 小時內取消可能酌收場域準備費。 遇颱風、地震、政府公告天災等不可抗力，將協助延期或全額退款。 Free changes or cancellation 7+ days before the visit; cancellation 3–7 days ahead incurs a 30% admin fee; within 72 hours, a site-preparation fee may apply. For force majeure such as typhoons, earthquakes, or officially declared disasters, we'll help reschedule or fully refund.
行動不便者可以參與嗎？Can guests with limited mobility join?
基地位於山區，部分步道與設施仍在無障礙改善中。 請於表單註明行動不便者人數與需求（輪椅、手杖、視聽障等）， 我們將為你客製可參與的動線與導覽方式。 竹徑文化講堂主體（一期）已優先納入無障礙設計。 The site is in the mountains, and some trails and facilities are still being made accessible. Please note in the form the number and needs of guests with limited mobility (wheelchair, cane, hearing/vision, etc.), and we'll customize an accessible route and guiding method for you. The main Bamboo Path Cultural Hall (Phase 1) already prioritizes accessible design.
是否提供企業 ESG 可揭露資料？Do you provide disclosable corporate ESG data?
是的。企業 ESG 參訪方案包含： 減碳量估算（基於太陽能 + 高溫窯節能）、 竹林撫育公頃數、培訓人次、 Saisiyat Raromaeh 品牌授權選項， 與 SDGs 8 / 12 / 13 / 15 對齊清單， 可直接納入企業永續報告書揭露。詳細資料包請與合作社洽談。 Yes. The corporate ESG visit plan includes: carbon-reduction estimates (based on solar + high-temperature kiln efficiency), hectares of bamboo tended, number of people trained, Saisiyat Raromaeh brand-licensing options, and an alignment checklist with SDGs 8 / 12 / 13 / 15 — ready for inclusion in your sustainability report. Contact the co-op for the full data pack.
