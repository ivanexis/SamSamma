關於山莎蔓岸About SamSamma

從一個名字、一片竹林、一群族人開始，慢慢長成現在的合作社。 From a name, a bamboo grove, and a community — slowly grown into the co-op of today.

「山莎蔓岸（SamSama:an）」為賽夏族語，意指「一年四季湧出水源、綿延流動之地」，象徵生命、生產與文化生生不息的力量。山莎蔓岸位於新竹縣五峰鄉大隘地區，屬於北賽夏（Say Kilapa）傳統聚落範圍，長久以來族人依循山林節奏生活，以竹林、農耕、祭儀與部落共享文化維繫族群發展。其中，「竹」不僅是建築、農具與生活器物的重要材料，更是祭儀文化、集體勞動與傳統知識傳承的重要媒介，承載著賽夏族與土地共生共榮的文化記憶。 "SamSamma:an" is a Saisiyat word meaning "the place where water springs year-round and flows continuously," symbolizing the enduring power of life, production, and culture. Located in Daai, Wufeng Township, Hsinchu County, SamSamma lies within the traditional settlement area of Northern Saisiyat (Say Kilapa). For generations, our people have lived to the rhythm of the forest—sustaining the community through bamboo groves, farming, rituals, and shared cultural life. Bamboo is not only a vital material for buildings, tools, and daily objects; it is also the medium for ritual culture, collective labor, and the passing of traditional knowledge, carrying the Saisiyat people's cultural memory of living in harmony with the land.

然而，隨著產業轉型與社會變遷，部落逐漸面臨青年外流、竹產業沒落、竹林荒廢、高齡化及文化傳承斷層等挑戰。2019 年起，在明新科技大學 USR（大學社會責任）計畫陪伴下，部落開始重新思考「如何讓竹林重新成為部落的資產，而非負擔」，並透過多次部落會議與集體討論，逐步形成以竹文化為核心、以合作社為組織、以永續生活為目標的發展方向。 Yet as industry shifts and society changes, the village has gradually faced youth outflow, the decline of the bamboo industry, abandoned groves, an aging population, and gaps in cultural inheritance. Since 2019, accompanied by Minghsin University of Science and Technology's USR (University Social Responsibility) program, the village began rethinking "how to make bamboo forests an asset rather than a burden," and through repeated village meetings and collective discussion, gradually formed a direction centered on bamboo culture, organized as a cooperative, and aimed at sustainable living.

2024 年，族人共同成立「有限責任新竹縣山莎蔓岸竹木產業生產合作社」，以竹林經營、竹炭製造、竹醋液開發、竹工藝及文化體驗等多元模式，建立部落自主經濟體系。同時，藉由產業發展重新連結賽夏族竹文化知識、傳統工藝、tinato' 祭儀精神與土地倫理，使文化成為產業的核心價值。此外，合作社亦積極吸引青年返鄉，結合設計、行銷、數位科技及經營管理等專業能力，促進跨世代合作與創新。 In 2024, community members jointly established "Limited Liability Hsinchu County SamSamma Bamboo Industry Production Co-op," building an autonomous tribal economy through bamboo forest management, bamboo charcoal production, bamboo vinegar development, bamboo crafts, and cultural experiences. At the same time, this development reconnected Saisiyat bamboo knowledge, traditional craftsmanship, the tinato' ritual spirit, and land ethics, making culture the core value of the enterprise. The cooperative also actively attracts returning youth by combining design, marketing, digital technology, and management expertise to promote intergenerational collaboration and innovation.

山莎蔓岸不僅是一個部落名稱，更是一場以文化復振、產業創新與部落自主為核心的永續實踐，展現賽夏族重新找回土地、文化與未來發展主權的共同願景。 SamSamma is more than a village name; it is a sustainable practice rooted in cultural revitalization, industrial innovation, and tribal autonomy—demonstrating the Saisiyat people's shared vision of reclaiming land, culture, and future development sovereignty.

五月，攜手籌備前往日本的竹工藝展May: preparing together for a bamboo-craft exhibition in Japan

2026 年 5 月｜大隘部落紀錄May 2026 | Daai Tribe journal

最近，園區正為了一項即將遠行的計畫而充滿期待。透過明新科大的計畫推動，我們即將把臺灣優美的竹藝品帶往日本交流。 Lately the site has been full of anticipation for a journey ahead. Through a program led by Minghsin University of Science and Technology (MUST), we are about to bring Taiwan's beautiful bamboo crafts to Japan for exchange.

這次展演匯聚了跨界的力量，由技藝精湛的專業工藝師傾注心力操刀。看著他們熟練地將竹篾交織， 展現深厚的工藝底蘊，園區的夥伴們則化身幕後推手，從旁協助籌劃與連結資源。這是一場串聯技藝與心意的合作 —— 我們將這份職人精神帶上飛機，帶去京都，帶給願意看見的世界。 This showcase brings together cross-disciplinary forces, crafted with devotion by master artisans. Watching them skillfully weave bamboo strips, revealing deep craftsmanship, the site's members became the behind-the-scenes hands — helping plan and connect resources. It is a collaboration of skill and heart —— we carry this artisan spirit onto the plane, to Kyoto, to a world willing to see.

五月底，這四件作品將在京都府龜岡市的工藝展，被遠方的人輕輕拿起、慢慢端詳。 In late May, these four works will be gently picked up and admired by people far away, at a craft exhibition in Kameoka, Kyoto.

A Name Born of Water and Bamboo

四季湧泉，與竹同生Where springs flow year-round, and bamboo lives with us

「山莎蔓岸（SamSama:an）」是賽夏族語， 意指「一年四季湧出水源、綿延流動之地」── 象徵生命、生產與文化生生不息的力量。 我們位於新竹縣五峰鄉大隘地區， 屬於北賽夏（Say Kilapa）傳統聚落範圍。 "SamSama:an" is a Saisiyat word for "the place where water springs year-round and flows on without end" — the enduring power of life, production, and culture. 第二幕 · 族人Act Two · The People
By the People, of the Land

族人，是這個地方真正的主人Our people are the true owners of this place

山莎蔓岸是部落自己成立的合作社， 發起人是世代居住於大隘部落的賽夏族人。 所有獲利留在部落、所有故事都是親身經歷 — 這裡沒有演員、也沒有表演動作， 你看到的是這片土地上真正在生活的人。 SamSamma is a co-op founded by the tribe itself, started by Saisiyat people who have lived in the Daai tribe for generations. All profit stays in the tribe, and every story is lived firsthand — there are no actors and no staged performances; what you see are real people who actually live on this land.

四位族人取得專業證照， 文化傳承不是給遊客看的劇場，是日常的師徒制傳承。 Four community members hold professional licenses; cultural heritage here isn't theater for tourists, but everyday master-to-apprentice transmission.

USR · 大學走進部落University meets tribe
第三幕 · 學生Act Three · The Students
Students In, Co-op Out

學生走進部落，部落走進大學Students step into the tribe; the tribe steps into the university

從 2024 年開始，明新科大的學生陸續來到這裡 — 他們蹲在地上削竹篾、爬上樹屋鋪屋頂、 在會議室裡跟族人一起報告心得。 Since 2024, students from MUST have come here one after another — crouching on the ground to shave bamboo strips, climbing tree houses to lay roofs, and presenting reflections alongside the tribe in the meeting room.

合作社和明新科大整合了 永續設計、文創、商管、原民研究四個系所的老師， 把竹林變成大學社會責任實踐基地 (USR)。 這不是來打卡的場域，是被列入課程的真實學習現場。 Together with MUST we brought in faculty from four departments — sustainable design, cultural-creative industries, business management, and indigenous studies — turning the bamboo forest into a University Social Responsibility (USR) base. It's not a check-in spot, but a real learning site written into the curriculum.

ESG · 企業共榮Corporate partnership
第四幕 · 企業Act Four · The Partners
Where Carbon Becomes Community

企業夥伴，把採購變成共榮Corporate partners turning procurement into shared prosperity

合作社把竹林碳匯、循環經濟、部落採購結合 ── 企業夥伴來這裡上一堂低碳課、員工親手做一件竹炭作品、 再把對話和體驗，寫進 ESG 報告書最真實的那一頁。 SamSamma weaves together bamboo carbon sinks, the circular economy, and tribal procurement — corporate partners come for a low-carbon class, employees make a bamboo-charcoal piece by hand, and the conversation becomes the most authentic page in their ESG report.

採購這些竹炭商品，不只是「買綠色禮盒」── 是讓部落產業有現金流、讓族人留在山上、 讓「四季湧泉」這個名字繼續流動下去。 Buying these bamboo-charcoal goods isn't just "purchasing a green gift box" — it's giving tribal industry cash flow, keeping our people on the mountain, and letting the name "four-season springs" keep flowing.

第五幕 · 世界Act Five · The World
From Daai Village to Kyoto

我們的竹編，正在出國Our bamboo weaving is going abroad

2026 年 5 月，族人親自帶著作品飛到日本京都， 在當地工藝展現場用賽夏族傳統技法展示給日本朋友看。 這些不是「複製品」、不是「禮物」， 是部落師傅在大隘的竹林裡用整個四月編出來的作品。 In May 2026, our people personally flew their works to Kyoto, Japan, demonstrating Saisiyat traditional techniques to Japanese friends at a local craft exhibition. These are not "replicas" or "gifts" — they are works the tribe's masters wove over the whole of April, in the bamboo forests of Daai.

同年，京都府龜岡市簽署了合作意向書 ── 山莎蔓岸和京都的竹工藝師傅， 開始討論一場可能持續好多年的交流， 還有 2026 日本綠化博覽會的參展計畫。 That same year, Kameoka, Kyoto signed a letter of intent ── SamSamma and Kyoto's bamboo-craft masters began discussing an exchange that may last for many years, along with a plan to exhibit at the 2026 Japan Green Expo.

Q1. 我們是學校團體（20-50 人），最少要提前多久預約？Q1. We're a school group (20-50 ppl) — how far in advance should we book?

建議提前 7-14 天預約，學校團體需於行前 3 天提供保險投保資料；雨天備案請事先確認室內動線。We recommend booking 7-14 days ahead. School groups must provide insurance docs 3 days before the visit; please confirm rain-backup indoor routes in advance.

Q2. 企業 ESG 報告需要 carbon 數據與低碳活動證明，能附嗎？Q2. Our ESG report needs carbon data & low-carbon activity proof — can you provide it?

可開立統編、提供活動 carbon 估算與部落採購憑證；ESG 參訪可掛貴司案名，並出具參與證明書供 ESG 報告書引用。We can issue tax-ID invoices, provide carbon estimates & tribal-procurement records; ESG visits can carry your firm's project name with participation certificates for ESG reporting.

Q3. 雨天怎麼辦？取消或改期政策？Q3. What if it rains? Cancellation & rescheduling policy?

提供室內竹編、文化解說、工藝師講座等雨備方案；提前 7 天可免費改期；颱風或政府公告停班停課日全額退費。Indoor alternatives include weaving, cultural talks, & artisan lectures. Free reschedule with 7-day notice. Full refund on typhoon days or government-announced closures.

Q4. 餐食安排？有素食、過敏、兒童餐選項嗎？Q4. Meal arrangements? Vegetarian, allergy, or kids' menu options?

部落風味餐含素食選項；預約時請備註過敏原（如：堅果、海鮮）；兒童餐可客製化，建議行前 5 天確認人數與特殊需求。Tribal-style meals include vegetarian options. Please note allergens (e.g., nuts, seafood) when booking. Kids' meals are customizable — confirm headcount & special needs 5 days in advance.

Q5. 交通與停車？導航能直接到嗎？Q5. How about transport & parking? Can I just use GPS navigation?

山區產業道路，導航前請先致電確認；大型遊覽車（30 人以上）需事先確認路線；園區提供有限停車位，建議共乘或於山下集合接駁。Mountain industrial road — please call before navigating. Coach buses (30+) need route confirmation. Limited parking on-site — consider carpooling or gather at the foothill for shuttle.

Q6. 不會編竹子可以參加嗎？小孩可以嗎？Q6. Can I join if I've never woven bamboo? Are kids welcome?

完全 OK！工藝師會從入門教起，所有素材與工具皆已備齊；6 歲以上孩童適合參加，由家長陪同；過程中工藝師全程在旁協助。Absolutely! Artisans teach from scratch with all materials & tools ready. Kids ages 6+ are welcome with parental company; artisans assist throughout the session.
