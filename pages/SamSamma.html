00 · The Origin

為什麼是這個地方？ Why this place?

這不是一個突然開始的專案 ── 5 年前的計劃書裡，已經寫下今天還在解的問題。 This isn't a project that just started — five years ago, an undergraduate thesis already named the question being answered today.

老家在新竹縣五峰鄉南口，賽夏族大隘部落。
父執輩 60 年代北漂台北，與家鄉的連結近乎中斷。
2021 大學畢業設計第一次認真把這個部落當研究對象 ──
計劃書裡的一句話之後 5 年一直沒放下： Hometown: Nankou, Wufeng Township, Hsinchu — the Saisiyat Daai tribal village.
The previous generation moved to Taipei in the 1960s; the connection with the homeland was nearly cut.
In 2021, an undergraduate thesis was the first serious attempt to take this village as a research subject —
and one sentence from that thesis has stayed for five years:

「珍貴的文化資源正隨著部落耆老凋零。」 "The precious cultural resources are vanishing with the elders."

畢業設計後人未離開，伴隨合作社籌組成立逐步加入 ──
天時、地利、人和湊齊之後，才接續到 2026 年教育部 USR NT$1.45M 計畫（執行率 100%），
以及 2026/06/10 進入原民會評審的 NT$12M 兩年期計畫提案：
「賽夏族山莎蔓岸低碳竹產業聚落建構計畫 ── 竹林治理、空間整合、品牌共創與高值循環產業推升」。 Staying after graduation, gradually joining the co-op as it took shape —
the timing, the place, and the people aligned, leading to the FY2026 MOE USR NT$1.45M plan (100% execution),
and a NT$12M two-year proposal that entered the CIP review on 2026-06-10:
"Saisiyat SamSamma Low-Carbon Bamboo Industry Cluster — bamboo governance, spatial integration, co-created branding & high-value circular industry."

01 · The Problem

當時面對的現場The reality on the ground

這個專案最難的不是「做」一件事，而是「同時」處理四個看起來毫不相關的爛攤子。 下面這四張卡片，是我接手時親眼看見、親手觸碰的真實困境 ── 沒有人盤點過的竹林、各做各的村落、被瓦斯爐鎖死的產線、賣不出去的好東西。 The hardest part wasn't doing one thing — it was handling four seemingly unrelated messes at once. The four cards below are the real difficulties I saw and touched firsthand when I came on board: a forest no one had ever measured, villages each doing their own thing, a production line capped by a single gas kiln, and great products no one could buy.

PAIN 01

沒有人盤點過的 12 公頃竹林12 hectares of forest no one had ever measured

No one ever measured the forest
情境Context 族人從祖父輩開始種竹，但「到底有多少棵、能吸多少碳」沒人說得出來The community has grown bamboo since their grandparents' generation, yet no one could say how many stems there were or how much carbon they captured
為什麼難Why it's hard 沒有基線，就沒辦法跟企業講「我的竹林值多少」Without a baseline, there's no way to tell enterprises "what my bamboo forest is worth"
場域的缺口The gap on the ground 需要一份「可量化、可驗證」的數據，否則談 ESG 都是空話We needed quantifiable, verifiable data — otherwise any ESG talk was empty
處理方式Approach 引進空拍 + RTK 多光譜技術，建立 7,526 m² 樣區 (胸徑 5.7 cm、密度 19,200 株/ha、單位碳儲量 77.3 tC/ha) ── 從這裡開始，竹林才「有數字」可以講話 Introduced aerial + RTK multispectral surveying to build a 7,526 m² sample plot (DBH 5.7 cm, density 19,200 stems/ha, carbon storage 77.3 tC/ha) — only then did the forest finally have numbers to speak with
能力留存Retention 同步培力 4 位族人取得無人機操作證照 ── 部落本身具備持續更新 baseline 的能力，知識留在現場 Trained 4 community members to obtain drone operator certifications — the village now has the in-house capability to update the baseline themselves; knowledge stays on the ground
PAIN 02

四個村，各做各的Four villages, each going its own way

Four villages, four agendas
情境Context 五峰鄉四村，地理相連但各做各的，誰也說服不了誰Four villages in Wufeng are geographically connected but each does its own thing, with no one able to persuade the others
為什麼難Why it's hard 竹農、加工者、工藝者、民宿主，平常靠交情臨時動員，沒有制度Bamboo farmers, processors, craftspeople, and B&B owners mobilized ad hoc through personal ties — no system in place
場域的缺口The gap on the ground 沒有任何「決策怎麼做、誰分多少、利潤怎麼回到部落」的共識No consensus on how decisions get made, how shares are split, or how profit flows back to the tribe
處理方式Approach 發起合作社治理平台、建立跨村協作名冊 (≥ 25 人/戶) ── 把「靠人情」升級為「靠制度」，並設立收益回流公共基金 Launched a co-op governance platform and a cross-village collaboration roster (≥ 25 people/households) — upgrading "relying on goodwill" to "relying on systems," plus a public revenue-return fund
能力留存Retention 同步建立 跨村合作備忘錄、收益回流公共基金制度 ── 把「某一次合作」升級為「常態化治理單位」，制度留在合作社 Established cross-village MoUs and a public-fund revenue-flow-back system — moving "a single collaboration" into a "standing governance unit"; the rules stay with the co-op
PAIN 03

被瓦斯爐鎖死的天花板A ceiling locked by a single gas kiln

A ceiling made of a single gas kiln
情境Context 老一輩留下的瓦斯爐只能燒到 600°C，做出來的竹炭只能賣最低階的價The elders' gas kiln only reached 600°C, so the bamboo charcoal could only fetch the lowest-grade price
為什麼難Why it's hard 每次砍竹剩下的枝梢、邊角料就直接燒掉，可惜也賺不到Leftover branches and offcuts from each harvest were simply burned — wasteful and unprofitable
場域的缺口The gap on the ground 沒有高溫窯、沒有前處理設備，產線就只能停在這層樓Without a high-temperature kiln or pre-processing equipment, the production line was stuck on this floor
處理方式Approach 提案兩座機械爐升級至 900-1000°C、導入零廢棄 SOP，搭配 10 kW 太陽能與 1 噸淨水循環 ── 從「燒炭」升級為「碳負循環產線」 Proposed upgrading two mechanical kilns to 900–1000°C, introduced a zero-waste SOP, paired with 10 kW solar and a 1-tonne water-recycling loop — upgrading "burning charcoal" into a "carbon-negative circular production line"
能力留存Retention 同步出版 零廢棄產線 SOP + 設備維保手冊 + 操作訓練教材 ── 即使主要操作者離開，產線仍可運轉，SOP 留在窯邊 Documented a zero-waste production-line SOP, equipment maintenance manual, and operator training materials — even if the lead operator leaves, the line keeps running; the SOP stays at the kiln
PAIN 04

賣不出去的好東西Great products that couldn't be sold

A great story no one could buy
情境Context 賽夏文化、raromaeh 互助精神都很動人，但企業要採購時找不到「窗口」Saisiyat culture and the raromaeh spirit of mutual aid are moving, but enterprises wanting to buy couldn't find a single point of contact
為什麼難Why it's hard 工藝戶、民宿戶各賣各的，沒有聚落級對外的統一語言Craft households and B&Bs each sold on their own — no unified, cluster-level external messaging
場域的缺口The gap on the ground 企業 ESG 採購需要「一份合約、一個對口」，現在連這個都沒有Enterprise ESG procurement needs "one contract, one contact" — and even that didn't exist yet
處理方式Approach 建立「Saisiyat Raromaeh」共用品牌、做 ESG 採購提案包 (企業低碳禮盒 / ESG 參訪 / 永續採購) ── 讓部落第一次有「一份對外提案」可以遞出去 Built the "Saisiyat Raromaeh" shared brand and an ESG procurement package (corporate low-carbon gift sets / ESG visits / sustainable sourcing) — giving the tribe its first ever external proposal to hand out
能力留存Retention 同步制定 Saisiyat Raromaeh 母品牌治理架構（授權辦法 + 品質規範 + 聯名規則） ── 個別工藝戶申請即可上架，制度留在合作社 Built a Saisiyat Raromaeh master-brand governance framework (licensing rules + quality standards + co-branding system) — individual artisans can plug in on application; the framework stays with the co-op
02 · The Execution · 對齊目標Aligning Goals

Roadmap 桌上做的事：把抽象目標拆成可追的進度At the roadmap table: breaking abstract goals into trackable progress

「地方創生」太大、太模糊，沒人扛得動。團隊討論時把它拆成 治理 → 空間 → 資源 → 產線 → 品牌 五件具體事， 再切成分階段、每季有交付物的節奏 ── 第一階段先把基礎制度立起來，第二階段才談量產與上市。 這樣公部門看得到進度，族人也知道下一步該做什麼。 "Regional revitalization" is too big and vague for anyone to carry. In team discussions I helped break it into five concrete tracks — Governance → Space → Resources → Production → Brand — then sliced them into phased, quarter-by-quarter deliverables: Phase 1 stands up the foundational systems; Phase 2 tackles scale-up and go-to-market. This way the government sees progress, and the community knows what's next.

02 · The Execution · 溝通機制Communication

讓三個世界的人，坐在同一張桌子Getting three different worlds to sit at the same table

這個專案最難的不是技術，是「翻譯」。 地方長輩聽不懂教授的學術術語、教授看不懂部落的人情脈絡、 公部門只認得自己的格式。 身為 PM 我做的事，就是讓他們「對著同一份目標」說話 ── 下面這三軸，是我每天在切換的三種語言。 The hardest part of this project wasn't technology — it was "translation." Local elders couldn't follow professors' academic jargon, professors couldn't read the tribe's social fabric, and the government only recognized its own formats. As PM, my job was to get them all speaking toward the same goal — the three axes below are the three languages I switched between every day.

03 · The Impact

這些努力，最後交出了什麼？What did all this effort actually deliver?

公部門的指標看「合規」、PM 看「能不能用」。 團隊這段期間的努力，整理成下面三件可以對外回答的事： 數據儀表板、五個面向的目標達成、SDGs / ESG 對接。 每一個數字背後，都有一份可以驗收的交付物。 Government metrics measure "compliance"; a PM measures "is it usable." The team's work over this period boils down to three things we can answer externally: a data dashboard, goal attainment across five dimensions, and SDG / ESG alignment. Behind every number sits an acceptance-ready deliverable.

04 · The Bridge

這些經驗，為什麼可以平移到科技大廠Why this experience transfers to a big tech company

策略漂亮，現場才知道哪裡會卡。 下面這三段是真實踩過的坑 ── 跟長輩講不通、補助錢不夠用、教授和部落不同頻。 每一段都誠實記下「當時做了什麼、結果如何、現在回頭看哪裡可以更好」。 這些經驗本質上和大廠軟體 PM 處理的問題是一樣的: 跨部門協作、利害關係人對齊、在資源受限下達標。 點擊任一張卡片展開細節。 A strategy looks great until the field shows you where it breaks. The three stories below are real pitfalls I hit — failing to get through to elders, grants running short, professors and the tribe on different wavelengths. For each, I honestly note what I did, how it turned out, and what I'd do better in hindsight. These are fundamentally the same problems a big-tech software PM handles: cross-functional collaboration, stakeholder alignment, and hitting targets under constraints. 01
用「里程碑誘因」搞定偏鄉工班的點工困局
SKILL · Incentive Design Vendor Mgmt Budget Control
SSituation

偏鄉工人不接統包（Fixed-price）合約，堅持日薪點工結算。為了多領工資刻意拖延進度，導致施工品質下滑、預算開始失控。

TTask

在「不破壞地方關係」的前提下，重新設計合作模式，把進度與品質拉回軌道，並守住預算。

AAction

導入「里程碑激勵機制（Milestone-based Incentive）」，把工程拆成週查核點（如本週完成地基灌漿），準時／提前達標即結清該段費用 + 達標獎金。同時部署「可視化進度看板（Visual Kanban）」貼在部落會議室，建立工班榮譽感。

RResult

工期追回 15%，預算超支風險鎖定在可控範圍。學到 PM 在資源受限環境下，必須具備「因地制宜的利益協調能力」，不能只靠合約條文壓人。

現在回頭看： 真正讓工班動起來的不是「合約條文」，是「他們看得到自己的進度」。 這跟科技大廠跨部門協作很像 ── 強壓 KPI 沒用， 讓工程師看見自己的 ticket 從 backlog 移到 done，比 1000 句催促有效。
02
獨家廠商換不掉？用「規格降維 + 學術施壓」破局
SKILL · Single-source Risk Scope De-scoping Resource Leverage
SSituation

研發竹炭爐的合作企業進度嚴重落後、炭質達不到商業化標準。但因在地深厚人脈與技術獨佔性，戰略上「無法汰換」。

TTask

在不換廠商的前提下，限縮其影響範圍、引進外部資源施壓、保住計畫期程。

AAction

(1) 規格降維（Scope De-scoping）：把 2026 年交付物降為「核心功能 MVP 驗證」，先確保最基礎炭化功能，把研發風險分攤到 2027 年。
(2) 跨界資源槓桿：邀請明新科大教授團隊以「第三方技術顧問」身份進入產線進行技術審計（Audit），用溫度曲線、多孔隙率等客觀科學數據建立每日 Debug 日誌。

RResult

合作企業在學術數據佐證下，無法再推諉是原料問題，被迫調整研發方向。最終在「沒有更換廠商」的前提下，將研發時程導回正軌，並在期末產出符合經濟效益的常備炭品質。

現在回頭看： 這段我真正學到的是：當你換不掉一個夥伴，就要學會「縮小他的破壞半徑」。 這跟大廠處理「無法更換的供應商」或「歷史包袱重的舊系統」邏輯一樣 ── 隔離風險、用外部資源建立客觀標準、別讓單點故障拖垮整個專案。
01
把「碳匯經濟」翻譯成「設備跟青農補助」拿下長輩同意書Translating "carbon-sink economics" into "equipment and youth-farmer subsidies" to win the elders' consent
SKILL · User-Centric Comm Value Visualization Trust Building
SSituation

對外談 ESG 碳匯、數位資產，部落長輩聽不懂，甚至擔心土地產權被外人奪走，前期推動完全卡關。Talking about ESG carbon sinks and digital assets, the tribal elders couldn't follow — some even feared outsiders would seize their land titles, so early progress stalled completely.

TTask

在短時間內取得高齡地主信任，順利簽下 12 公頃竹林的盤點同意書。Quickly earn the trust of elderly landowners and secure their consent to survey the 12-hectare forest.

AAction

採取「利益具象化（Value Visualization）」策略：直接帶無人機航拍 3D 圖資到長輩家中，攤開地圖指著熟悉的溪流與竹林，用最直白的語言轉譯——「這台無人機拍您這塊地，明年政府審查通過，就能直接幫部落爭取到割草機設備，還能有經費把在外打工的青農留下來當巡山員。」I used a "value visualization" strategy: bringing drone 3D aerial imagery straight to the elders' homes, spreading out the map, pointing to familiar streams and bamboo groves, and translating in the plainest terms — "This drone mapped your land; once the government approves next year, it can secure mower equipment for the tribe and fund bringing young farmers home to work as forest rangers."

RResult

原本僵持數週的局面在 3 天內破冰，順利拿下關鍵地主 100% 同意書。學到頂級 PM 不是展現技術，而是把複雜技術「降維成用戶有感的實質價值」。A weeks-long stalemate broke in 3 days, securing 100% consent from the key landowners. The lesson: a top PM doesn't show off technology — they reduce complex tech into tangible value the user actually feels.

現在回頭看：Looking back: 這是最深刻的一課 ── 「不能用用戶的語言講話，再好的方案也賣不出去」。 這不只適用部落長輩，也適用對非技術背景的高層或客戶。 把 ESG 翻譯成「割草機 + 留青農」， 和把 API 翻譯成「使用者打開 App 後 0.3 秒看到結果」，是同一件事。 This was my deepest lesson — "if you can't speak the user's language, even the best solution won't sell." It applies not just to tribal elders, but to non-technical executives and clients too. Translating ESG into "mowers + keeping young farmers home" is the same act as translating an API into "the user sees a result 0.3 seconds after opening the app."
02
主動申請林業署疏伐案，做第二條資金曲線Proactively applying for a Forestry Agency thinning grant as a second funding curve
SKILL · Alt-Funding Stacking Grants Sustainability
SSituation

跟團隊一起算 Roadmap 時發現：單一補助來源沒辦法支撐 12 公頃竹林長期人工疏伐的成本，補助一結束、整個專案的維護就會斷掉，商業模式不健康。While running the roadmap numbers with the team, we found a single grant couldn't sustain the long-term cost of manually thinning 12 hectares of bamboo — once the grant ended, maintenance would collapse. The business model wasn't healthy.

TTask

協助團隊找出第二條資源來源，讓專案具備「自我造血能力」，擴大影響力與可持續性。Help the team find a second funding source so the project could "sustain itself," expanding impact and durability.

AAction

盤點各部會的補助方向，找到林業署（前林務局）有「在地林業班維護與疏伐」這條專屬標案。後續團隊一起把原民會「聚落建構」與林業署「森林資源永續」做戰略綁定，組合跨部會的資源去支應疏伐成本。I surveyed grant directions across ministries and found the Forestry Agency had a dedicated tender for "local forestry-crew maintenance and thinning." The team then strategically bound the Indigenous Council's "cluster building" with the Forestry Agency's "sustainable forest resources," combining cross-ministry resources to cover thinning costs.

RResult

(1) 成本降低：用林業署經費解決原民會人工維護成本過高的痛點。
(2) 品質提升：透過林業班專業疏伐，讓竹林生長品質達到常備炭商業規格。
(3) 生態系雙贏：保護地方樹木資源 + 創造在地就業。(1) Lower cost: Forestry Agency funds solved the Indigenous Council's high manual-maintenance cost.
(2) Higher quality: professional thinning by the forestry crew brought bamboo growth up to commercial charcoal grade.
(3) Ecosystem win-win: protecting local tree resources + creating local jobs.

現在回頭看：Looking back: 「單一資源養不起整個計畫」是團隊一起算出來的事實， 我當時做的是「主動去查還有什麼別的政策可以接」 ── 這跟大廠 PM 規劃產品多元化營收結構（廣告 + 訂閱 + 企業版）的思維是一樣的， 不能把專案的命壓在單一資源上。 "A single source can't sustain the whole project" was a fact the team computed together; what I did was proactively research what other policies we could tap — the same thinking a big-tech PM applies when designing a diversified revenue mix (ads + subscription + enterprise). You can't pin a project's survival on one source.
03
產學語言對不上部落？建立「顧問會議＋翻譯層」Academic jargon not landing with the tribe? Build an "advisory meeting + translation layer"
SKILL · Cross-discipline Agile Alignment Knowledge Transfer
SSituation

明新科大化材／企管／土木／休閒等 4 領域教授團隊提供的是「學術技術語言」（NDVI、SROI、ESG），但部落執行端聽到的是「機器要怎麼用？這個月要做什麼？」MUST's professors across 4 fields (chem. eng. / business / civil / leisure) spoke in "academic-technical language" (NDVI, SROI, ESG), but the tribe's execution side was asking "How do I use this machine? What do we do this month?"

TTask

讓產學知識能真正落地，避免一次性 USR 計畫結束後技術隨教授離場而失傳。Make the academic knowledge truly land, so the tech wouldn't disappear with the professors once the one-off USR program ended.

AAction

建立「顧問團—工作包—交付成果」常態制度：每季 1 次聚落顧問會議、每年 4 次現地輔導，並把每位教授的輔導內容拆解為可被部落操作的工作包（Work Package）—包含 SOP、訓練教材、設備維保手冊、結訓證明。PM 自己充當「技術語言 ↔ 部落語言」的翻譯層。Built a standing "advisors — work packages — deliverables" system: one quarterly cluster advisory meeting, four on-site coaching sessions a year, and broke each professor's guidance into tribe-operable work packages — SOPs, training materials, equipment-maintenance manuals, completion certificates. As PM, I personally served as the "technical language ↔ tribal language" translation layer.

RResult

把 USR 一次性成果，轉化為合作社可長期維運的 SOP、培力人次 230、證照取得 10 人，形成「自主運作的綠領團隊」。技術不再綁定在外部教授身上。Turned one-off USR outputs into long-term, co-op-maintainable SOPs, 230 training participant-times, and 10 licensed members — forming a "self-operating green-collar team." The know-how was no longer tied to external professors.

現在回頭看：Looking back: 這段最有感的，是「知識怎麼留下來」這件事。 教授會走、合約會結束、但 SOP / 教材 / 證照人才留下來才是真的。 這就跟科技大廠講的「知識管理 (Knowledge Transfer)」是同一件事 ── 你的工作不是讓自己變成不可取代，而是讓專案就算沒了你，也跑得起來。 What hit me most here was the question of "how knowledge stays." Professors leave, contracts end — but SOPs / materials / licensed talent staying is what's real. This is exactly what big tech calls "knowledge transfer" — your job isn't to make yourself irreplaceable, but to make the project run even without you.

Engagement with this site began with a 2021 undergraduate thesis and grew alongside the co-op's formation. A NT$1.45M MOE USR plan was completed (100% execution) in H1 of FY2026; a NT$12M two-year proposal entered the CIP review on 2026-06-10. On-the-ground role: Digital Transformation PM and frontend developer at the core of the discussions — responsible for structuring messy field data and single-handedly delivering the co-op site and zero-server reservation workflow.
